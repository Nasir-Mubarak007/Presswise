## Customization flow — lightweight progress

A different pattern on purpose: no numbered steps, no "Step 2 of 5" — just dots and one current-step word, so the live editor (upload → customize → specs → preview → order) doesn't compete for attention against the product preview itself. First-time orientation is handled separately, via a one-time onboarding walkthrough, not this.
